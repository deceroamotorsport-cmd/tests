Data Deletion Instructions · Fabrica DCM

Fabrica DCM · De Cero a Motorsport · Last updated: 6 November 2026

To delete the data Fabrica DCM stores about you (your Instagram-scoped ID, username and the comment or message that triggered an automatic reply), do one of the following:

  1. Send a direct message to @deceroamotorsport on Instagram with the word DELETE (or BORRAR), or
  2. Email deceroamotorsport@gmail.com with your Instagram username.

We delete it within 30 days and confirm it to you by the same channel.
