Privacy Policy · Fabrica DCM

Fabrica DCM · De Cero a Motorsport · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Fabrica DCM is an internal tool operated by Matías Écija Fernández (sole trader), trading as De Cero a Motorsport (United Kingdom). It is used only to manage the Instagram professional accounts owned by De Cero a Motorsport (@deceroamotorsport) and its founder (@matiasecija.motorsport). It is not offered to third parties and nobody else can log in.

What the app does

  • Publishes and schedules posts, reels and carousels on our own accounts.
  • Reads the comments on our own posts so we can read, reply to or hide them from our dashboard.
  • Sends an automatic reply to people who comment a keyword on our posts or send it to us by direct message (for example, sending a free guide to someone who comments "GUIDE"). Only the person who wrote the keyword receives it, and only once.
  • Reads aggregated statistics (reach, likes, saves) of our own posts.

Data we process

For people who interact with our accounts: the Instagram-scoped user ID, the username, the text of the comment or message that triggered an automatic reply, and the date. For our own accounts: the content we publish and its statistics.

We do not read other conversations, we do not build profiles, we do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Why

Only to deliver the reply the person asked for, to avoid sending it twice, and to moderate comments on our own posts. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering requests made to us, and the person's own request.

Retention

Up to 12 months, after which the data is deleted. Access tokens are kept only on that private computer, readable only by the app, and are never shared.

Security

The app runs on a private computer. Its dashboard is only reachable through a private network with a password. Its only public address is the Meta webhook endpoint. These legal pages are hosted separately on GitHub Pages.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it at any time: see Data Deletion or email deceroamotorsport@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
